Day 138 - 3rd Month

Hello baby,

Three months na agad tayo! Ang bilis ng panahon, pero every single day with you feels like a blessing. Excited ako makita ka today, to try new things together, eat new food, and visit places for the first time. Alam mo naman, halos lahat ng firsts natin, tayo ang kasama ng isa’t isa. And that makes everything extra special. Kahit simpleng lakad lang, basta ikaw ang kasama, it feels like an adventure.

Thank you, baby, for trusting and loving me these past three months. I know na hindi lang tayo hanggang dito — marami pa tayong adventures ahead, highs and lows, and matagal tayong magsasama, hanggang leeg di ba? Until the day we marry each other, until the day we welcome our little Asher and Elianna, until the time they have their own kids, until our hair turns gray, until our very last breath. I imagine us growing old together, holding hands kahit mahina na tayo, still laughing at our silly jokes (kahit madalas wala kang support sa jokes ko tapos “hehe” lang response mo), still calling each other “baby.”

Baby, I love you so much! I know our journey had challenges, pero nandito pa rin tayo, stronger than before. Alam ko na both of us made adjustments — sa ugali, sa decisions, sa actions — just to protect each other’s hearts. Choices we made, always for us. Thank you, hon, para don. All your efforts, patience, and sincerity, ramdam ko silang lahat, and I treasure them deeply.

You are my greatest blessing, my partner, my person, my constant, my forever. With you, I feel safe, loved, and complete. Every hug, every kiss, every smile from you reminds me that I am exactly where I’m meant to be. Kahit minsan may tampuhan, may misunderstandings, at may pagod, I know we’ll always choose each other. Because love is not just about the happy moments — it’s about staying, fighting, and growing together (paulit ulit na ako dito, but yeah, you know the drill). 

I promise to keep choosing you, baby, every single day. I promise to support your dreams, to listen to your heart, and to be your safe place. I promise to laugh with you, cry with you, and stand by you no matter what. I promise na kahit tumagal pa ng decades, hindi magbabago ang pagmamahal ko sayo — lalo pa itong lalalim, lalo pa itong titibay.

So here’s to us, baby. To more months, more years, and a lifetime of love together. To countless adventures, endless cuddles, and memories we’ll cherish forever. To the family we’ll build, the home we’ll create, and the love story we’ll continue writing.

I love you, baby. Always and forever.

- love, Ruuu
