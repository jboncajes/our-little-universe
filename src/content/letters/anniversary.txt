Day 411 - 1st Year

Hello, babyyyy!

Happy first anniversary to us, my love! ❤️

One whole year. Twelve months. 365 days of loving you, learning you, choosing you, and finding more and more reasons to be thankful that out of all the people in this world, somehow, I got to meet you. I got to know you. And somehow, I got to be the person you call yours.

Baby, do you know what I ask myself sometimes?

**What did I do to deserve you?**

I genuinely wonder about that, hon. What did I ever do to deserve someone like you? Someone who loves me the way you do. Someone who stays even when things are not easy. Someone who tries to understand me, even when I struggle to explain what I feel. Someone who gives me comfort, patience, and affection, even on days when I know I am not the easiest person to love.

Sometimes, I look at you and think about how lucky I am. Not just because I have a beautiful girlfriend, not just because I have someone to hold, kiss, and spend my days with, but because I have someone whose heart is so precious to me. Someone who makes me feel loved in ways I never want to get used to. Someone who makes me wonder how I ended up being this fortunate.

And the truth is, baby, I don't think I will ever have a perfect answer to that question. I don't know what I did to deserve you. I just know that I am so, so grateful that I get to love you and be loved by you.

Thank you for everything you do for me, even the things you probably think are small. Thank you for checking on me, for worrying about me, for taking care of me when I am sick, for thinking about my comfort, and for remembering the little things that matter to me. Thank you for listening to my stories, even the random ones that probably make no sense sometimes. Thank you for putting up with my jokes, my moods, my random decisions, and all the little things that make me who I am. Thank you for your patience when I need to understand something, for your honesty when something hurts, and for giving me the chance to understand you better.

I hope you know that I see those things, baby. I see the effort behind your actions. I see the love behind the things you do. I see the way you try, even when you are tired, busy, or dealing with your own worries. I see the heart behind your love, and I hope I never make you feel like any of it goes unnoticed.

Because, hon, you do so much more for me than you probably realize.

You make me feel cared for. You make me feel important. You make me feel like there is someone in this world who genuinely wants me to be okay. And there is something so beautiful about knowing that I have someone who thinks about me, who wants to hear about my day, who wants to be part of my little victories, and who cares about the things that weigh on my heart.

I never want to take that for granted.

I never want to become so used to your love that I forget how special it is to receive it. I never want the things you do for me to become ordinary just because you do them often. I want to keep noticing you. I want to keep appreciating you. I want to keep being amazed by the fact that I get to call you my baby.

Because if there is one thing this past year has taught me, it is that being loved by you is one of the greatest blessings I have ever received.

And I hope you know that I don't love you only because of how you make me feel. I love you for the person you are. I love your heart, your kindness, your silliness, your little habits, your dreams, and even the parts of yourself that you sometimes struggle to accept. I love the way you can be so sweet and so makulit at the same time. I love the way we can talk about something serious one minute and end up laughing at something completely ridiculous the next. I love that we can be ourselves around each other, without having to pretend to be someone else.

I love that I have a person I can share my life with, not just the beautiful parts, but also the messy, exhausting, and uncertain ones.

And baby, I know our first year was not always easy.

We had our misunderstandings. We had moments when we struggled to understand each other. There were times when our feelings got hurt, when our doubts became louder than our reassurance, and when we had to work harder to find our way back to each other. We have experienced things we never want to go through again, and there were moments when loving each other required more than just saying the words.

But look at us, hon. We made it to one year.

Not because everything went perfectly, but because we kept learning. We kept trying. We kept finding ways to understand each other, to make room for each other's feelings, and to remember why we chose this relationship in the first place.

And if you ever wonder whether you are enough for me, baby, I hope you remember this letter.

You do not have to be perfect to be loved by me. You do not have to have everything figured out. You do not have to be happy all the time, or strong all the time, or know exactly what to say whenever something feels wrong. You are allowed to have difficult days. You are allowed to feel tired, to feel unsure, to need reassurance, and to ask for comfort.

You are allowed to be human with me.

I don't want you to feel like you have to earn my love by always getting everything right. I want to love you through the days when you feel beautiful and the days when you struggle to see it yourself. I want to celebrate you when you are proud of yourself and remind you of your worth when you are not. I want to hold your hand when life feels exciting and hold it just as tightly when life feels frightening.

I want to be the person who reminds you that you are loved, especially on the days when you find it difficult to believe it.

Because you deserve that kind of love, baby. A love that doesn't make you question your worth. A love that makes room for your feelings. A love that listens, reassures, understands, and keeps choosing you. And if I have the privilege of being the person who gives you that love, I promise I will never stop trying to become better at it.

I know I have my own shortcomings. I know there are still things I need to learn and ways I need to grow. There will be times when I fall short, when I don't understand you right away, or when I struggle to express what is in my heart. But I want you to know that I will never stop wanting to be better for you and for us.

Not because you ask me to become someone else, but because loving you makes me want to become a better version of the person I already am.

I want to learn how to love you in ways that reach your heart. I want to understand the things you don't always say out loud. I want to notice when you need a little more affection, when you need someone to listen, and when you simply need me beside you. I want to make you feel as loved as you have made me feel.

And I want to keep doing that long after this first anniversary.

I want to celebrate our second year, our fifth, our tenth, and all the years we are blessed enough to share. I want to look back at this letter someday and smile at how young we were in this chapter of our lives. I want us to remember the places we have visited, the food we have tried, the firsts we have shared, the silly conversations, the long hugs, the kisses, and all those ordinary moments that became precious simply because we experienced them together.

I want to see us reach the dreams we talk about today. I want to celebrate your achievements and watch you become everything you have ever wanted to be. I want to build a life with you, a home where we can both rest, laugh, grow, and feel safe. I want the family we have dreamed about, the little Asher and Elianna we have talked about, and the quiet mornings when we wake up beside each other and realize that the life we once imagined has become our reality.

And when we are older, when our hair has turned gray and our bodies are no longer as strong as they are now, I hope I will still be holding your hand. I hope I will still be calling you baby. I hope we will still be laughing at our silly jokes, teasing each other over the smallest things, and finding new reasons to fall in love with each other.

I hope that when we look back at all the years behind us, we will be able to say that we did not just spend a lifetime together. We made that lifetime beautiful by choosing each other through it all.

But for now, baby, I just want to thank you for this first year.

Thank you for every hug that made me feel safe. Every kiss that made me feel loved. Every moment you stayed a little longer. Every time you checked if I was okay. Every effort you made to understand me. Every time you made space for me in your day, even when you had so much going on in your own life.

Thank you for letting me see the parts of you that not everyone gets to see. Thank you for trusting me with your feelings, your fears, your dreams, and your heart. Thank you for letting me be part of your life and for allowing me to love you this closely.

Thank you for being patient with me. Thank you for being gentle with me. Thank you for making me laugh. Thank you for making me feel like I have someone to come home to, even on days when all we can do is talk through a screen or wish we could be beside each other.

Thank you for choosing us, even when choosing us required effort.

And thank you, baby, for loving me.

I don't think you will ever fully understand how much that means to me. There is a part of me that still finds it unbelievable that someone like you can look at someone like me and see a person worth loving. That you can know my imperfections, witness my difficult days, see the parts of me I am still working on, and still want to be here.

Sometimes, I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, even just for a moment. Maybe then you would understand why I feel so blessed. Maybe then you would understand why I am so grateful for the way you love me. Maybe then you would see that you are not just someone who came into my life and made it happier. You are someone who has changed the way I imagine my future.

Before you, the future was something I would eventually face. Now, when I think about it, I find myself imagining your hand in mine. I imagine the places we have yet to visit, the dreams we have yet to achieve, and the years we have yet to spend together.

You have become part of the things I look forward to. Part of the prayers I whisper. Part of the happiness I hope for. Part of the life I want to build.

So, what did I do to deserve you?

Maybe I will spend the rest of my life wondering about that.

But if there is one thing I know, it is that I will spend the rest of my life being thankful for you.

I may never understand why I was blessed with a love as beautiful as yours, but I promise I will do my best to take care of it. I will take care of your heart. I will take care of the trust you give me. I will take care of the little world we have created together. I will never stop reminding you that you are wanted, appreciated, cherished, and loved.

And when life gets difficult, when we are tired, when we have to make hard decisions, and when the road ahead is not as easy as we hoped, I want us to remember this first year. I want us to remember that we have already learned so much about loving each other, and that we still have so much more to learn.

We don't need to have every answer today, baby. We just need to keep being honest with each other, keep listening, keep growing, and keep choosing to walk through life hand in hand.

One year down, my love. And if I am lucky enough to have you for all the years that follow, I know that this will only be the beginning of a beautiful story.

Happy first anniversary, my baby, my best friend, my favorite person, my love, and my future wife.

I would still choose the girl who makes me wonder what I did to deserve her.

I would still choose you, even if I had to find you all over again.

And if I am given a thousand lifetimes, I hope I get to spend every one of them finding my way back to you.

I love you more than I know how to explain, baby. More than this letter could ever hold. More than these words could ever show. And I hope that for as long as I am blessed to be by your side, I can spend every day showing you what those words mean.

Happy first year to us, babyyyy.

Thank you for being my answered prayer, my greatest blessing, and the love I will never stop being grateful for.

**I don't know what I did to deserve you, baby. But I know I will never stop thanking God that I have you.**
