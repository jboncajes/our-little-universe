Day 197 - 5th Month

Hi baby,

Happy 5th month sa atin! Grabe, ang bilis pero ang dami na nating napagdaanan together. Hindi man siya perfect, pero sobrang special ng bawat moment na nashare natin. I’m really, really thankful for these 5 months — sa lahat ng oras, effort, at pagmamahal na binibigay mo sa akin. Kung babalikan ko lahat, wala akong gustong baguhin, kasi bawat part nun, kasama ka.

Every time na nagkikita tayo, ibang level yung excitement nating dalawa, alam natin yan. Kahit ilang beses na tayong nagkita, hindi nagsasawa at hindi magsasawa yung puso ko. Kahit simpleng lakad, usap, o tahimik na moments lang, sobrang saya ko na basta ikaw yung kasama ko. You make everything feel light, calm, and worth it.

Tapos sa bawat tampuhan natin, kahit hindi laging madali, nagpapasalamat pa rin ako. Kasi doon tayo natututo — how to understand each other better, how to be more patient, and how to love each other the right way. Hindi man perfect, pero totoo, at lalong tumitibay.

Ang dami rin nating firsts na naranasan together, and I’ll always treasure those. From watching sunsets and sunrises, to being together rain or shine — lahat ng yun naging mas maganda dahil ikaw yung katabi ko. Kahit simpleng ilaw sa daan o fireworks sa langit, parang mas kumikinang kapag ikaw yung kasama ko. Every view becomes more beautiful with this beauty beside me — ikaw yun.

I love you in your highs, lalo na kapag proud ka sa sarili mo, at mas lalo kitang minamahal sa mga panahong hindi mo yun maramdaman. Kapag nagdududa ka sa sarili mo, or when you feel like you’re not enough, please remember na sobrang sapat ka para sa akin — more than enough, actually. I see you, I appreciate you, and I’m always here for you no matter what.

Thank you for choosing me, for loving me, and for staying kahit sa mga araw na hindi ako madali mahalin. I’m so lucky to have you, baby. And I promise to keep choosing you, every single day — sa good days, bad days, and everything in between.

I love you more than words can explain. Happy 5th month sa atin, hon! Mahal na mahal kita!

- love, Ruuu
