Day 77 - 1st Month

Hi baby, it's been a month, and somehow it feels both short and long in the most beautiful way. Short because it flew so fast and long because in just 30 days you've already become such a big, important part of my life. I still remember that moment clearly. How my happiness overflowed into tears. I am so blessed to be loved by you. Gentle. Safe. Real.

Our paths crossed in such an unplanned, almost impossible way, choosing each other, learning each other, dreaming little dreams together.

Every day with you feels like something I prayed for without even realizing it. And now that you're here, I don't ever want to take your love for granted. Never, baby. Liligawan every single day, making sure na palagi tayong sigurado sa isa't-isa.

Hindi man palaging masaya, I actually treasure that too, because it shows that what we have is not perfect, but it is honest and real. What matters is that we're trying, fixing, listening, and growing together. We learn from our misunderstanding. We soften in our differences. And even when things get heavy, we don't let go. We held on a little tighter.

If you ever think that our moods or our tampuhan will make me love you less, I'm sorry baby, but that will never be true. Those moments don't scare me. They make me want to understand you more, protect you more, love you more. You are still you on your worst or busiest or heaviest days, and that "you" is still the one I choose.

You make ordinary days feel special. You make silence feel comforting (kahit tulog ka lang sa tabi ko, promise). You make my heart feel at home. And in just one month, you've already left a mark so deep in me that I know you will be part of my life for a long looong time.

So here I am, a month in, loving you more deeply than yesterday, and ready to love you even more tomorrow. Thank you for being you. Thank you for choosing me. Thank you sa pag-intindi sa moods ko, keeping up with my random decisions, for loving me unconditionally. And thank you for this beautiful first month as yours.

Happy first month to us. Aya, Zaii, cutie, pretty, yeppeuni, baby, hon, wifey. Always you, always us.

- love, Ruuu
